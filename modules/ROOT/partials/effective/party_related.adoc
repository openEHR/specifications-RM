2+^h|*PARTY_RELATED*

2+a|Proxy type for identifying a party and its relationship to the subject of the record. Use where the relationship between the party and the subject of the record must be known.

2+|Inherits: xref:{class_party_proxy}[PARTY_PROXY], xref:{class_party_identified}[PARTY_IDENTIFIED]

2+h|*Attributes*

|*external_ref*: `xref:{class_party_ref}[PARTY_REF]` [0..1]
a|Optional reference to more detailed demographic or identification information for this party, in an external system. +
_Inherited from xref:{class_party_proxy}[PARTY_PROXY]_

|*name*: `link:/classes/String[String] ` [0..1]
a|Optional human-readable name (in String form). +
_Inherited from xref:{class_party_identified}[PARTY_IDENTIFIED]_

|*identifiers*: `link:/classes/List[List] <xref:{class_dv_identifier}[DV_IDENTIFIER]>` [0..1]
a|One or more formal identifiers (possibly computable). +
_Inherited from xref:{class_party_identified}[PARTY_IDENTIFIED]_

|*relationship*: `xref:{class_dv_coded_text}[DV_CODED_TEXT]` [1..1]
a|Relationship of subject of this ENTRY to the subject of the record. May be coded. If it is the patient, coded as  self.
